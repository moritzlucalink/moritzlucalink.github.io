How to play

Goal

Bring all 15 of your checkers into your home board, then bear them off. The first player to bear off all their checkers wins.

Direction

White moves from point 24 down to 1, and its home board is points 1–6. Black moves the other way round. The point numbers on the board are always shown from the view of the player whose turn it is.

Starting

Each player rolls one die. The higher roll starts and plays both dice. If the dice are equal, both roll again.

Moving

  • Each die moves one checker that many points. Doubles are played four times.
  • You may land on an empty point, on your own checkers, or on a single opposing checker, called a blot. Landing on a blot hits it and sends it to the bar.
  • You may not land on a point with two or more opposing checkers.
  • If you have checkers on the bar, you must bring them back first, into your opponent's home board.
  • You must use both dice if you can. If you can only use one, you must use the larger.

Bearing off

Once all your checkers are in your home board, you can take them off with the exact number. A higher number may take off a checker from your highest occupied point.

Scoring

  • Normal win: 1 point.
  • Gammon, 2 points: the loser has borne off no checkers.
  • Backgammon, 3 points: a gammon where the loser also still has a checker on the bar or in the winner's home board.

Controls

Tap a glowing checker, then a highlighted point. To bear off, tap the tray. Each move uses one die. Undo takes back moves until you press Done. Keyboard: R roll, U undo, Enter done, Esc deselect.

Board layout: Auto turns the board upright on tall screens like phones held upright. You can also fix it to Wide or Upright.
